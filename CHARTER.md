# OPEN Mission Charter

OPEN will always be free, transparent, and community-run. These terms are the foundation of the project:

- **Free:** no subscriptions, paid tiers, advertisements, or premium features, ever.
- **Transparent:** open-source code (AGPL for the platform, Apache-2.0 for SDKs and API specifications) and a published, versioned rating spec.
- **Community-run:** no person, company, or sponsor owns or controls it, including the founder.
- **Portable data:** players and leagues can export their data at any time.
- **One standard:** anyone can read and fork the code, but only the governing board changes the official spec, and only conforming tools can show an OPEN rating.

Everyone who takes part in OPEN agrees to this charter.

This charter can only be changed under the amendment rules in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/openpickleball/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#amending-the-charter).
