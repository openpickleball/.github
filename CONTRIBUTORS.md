## How to be added or removed

A maintainer adds you after your pull request is merged, or after a maintainer reworks your pull request or replaces it with a new one. Other help counts too, like feedback on the app or a review of the rating methods. If we missed you, ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/openpickleball/.github/discussions), or open a pull request that adds you to this file. Asking for yourself is fine.

To be removed, ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/openpickleball/.github/discussions), or open a pull request that takes you off. If you'd rather not ask in public, email **info@openpb.org**.

See the [contributing guide](https://github.com/openpickleball/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.
